# Voiceover v3, Sudanese, with figures

About 119 spoken words, roughly 58 seconds at a slow, serious pace. Lines marked (optional) can be dropped if the read runs long; their figure stays on screen.

## Script (to read aloud)

```
قريب أربعة سنين حرب... ورمضان قرّب، والصواني فاضية.
قريب أربعة وتلاتين مليون زول في السودان... محتاجين إغاثة.
تقريباً تلتين أهلنا.                                         (optional)
الأمم المتحدة بتقول: دي أكبر أزمة إنسانية في الدنيا.          (optional)
وقريب عشرين مليون زول... بيواجهوا الجوع.
وأكتر من تمنمية ألف شافع صغير... سوء التغذية مهدد حياتهم.

ورا كل رقم... أسرة كانت مستورة.
وفي أم... ما عارفة حتفطّر شُفّعها بشنو.
وبيوت كانت صينيتها بتمرق للضرا مليانة... هسع بتقفل بابها وتستر حالها.

الحرب ما في إيدنا... لكن الصينية في إيدنا.

اتكفّلوا بحقيبة رمضان... بخمسين دولار أو قيمتها، لأسرة فيها ستة أنفار، لأسبوعين.
بنكك، فوري، انستا باي، ولا من برّا مصر...
وبعد ما تحوّلوا، رسّلوا الإشعار في الواتساب.

الخير ما بكتر بمال زول واحد...
الخير بكتر بقلوب كتيرة اجتمعت على المحبة والعطاء.
```

## English

Nearly four years of war... Ramadan is coming, and the trays are empty.
Nearly 34 million people in Sudan... need aid.
Almost two-thirds of our people.
The UN says: this is the largest humanitarian crisis in the world.
Nearly 20 million people... are facing hunger.
And more than 800,000 small children... malnutrition threatens their lives.

Behind every number... a family that once lived with dignity.
A mother... who doesn't know what she'll break her children's fast with.
Homes whose tray used to go out full to the street iftar... now close their door and hide their need.

The war is not in our hands... but the tray is.

Sponsor a Ramadan bag... fifty dollars or its value, for a family of six, for two weeks.
Bankak, Fawry, InstaPay, or from outside Egypt...
And after you transfer, send the receipt on WhatsApp.

Goodness doesn't grow from one person's money...
It grows from many hearts gathered in love and giving.

## How to read it

Read it like you'd tell family hard news in a WhatsApp voice note: low, steady, close to the phone. Don't perform. The weight comes from slowing down, not from raising your voice. Each "..." is a real half-second pause.

1. Opening and figures: flat, quiet, plain. Slow down on each number and take a small breath after it. Leave a full beat of silence after "مهدد حياتهم".
2. "ورا كل رقم": the slowest line. Hold a second after "رقم" and let your voice fall on "مستورة".
3. The mother and the homes: softer, like talking about someone you know. Let "وتستر حالها" trail off.
4. Take a breath before "الحرب ما في إيدنا", keep it low, then lift and warm up on "لكن الصينية في إيدنا". This is where it turns to hope.
5. The ask and payment: warm, clear, direct. No sales voice.
6. The proverb: the slowest and most heartfelt read. Pause after "زول واحد", then say the second half as one phrase.
