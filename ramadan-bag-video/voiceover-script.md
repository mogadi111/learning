## Delivery

A real Sudanese voice beats text-to-speech here, ideally a warm female voice from the family, talking like a khala telling a story rather than an announcer. Use the Sudanese "g" for ق (حقيبة = ḥagība, قدر = gadur, قامت = gāmat, بقلوب = bi-gulūb).

The shape of the read:

1. 0–9.7s: warm and smiling, brisk enough to hook. "والشفع مترجين المغرب" said fondly.
2. 9.7–17s: breathe before "لكن", then slower and lower. Quiet and dignified, never tearful.
3. 17s: lift on "عشان الصينية تتملى تاني", pause, then "دي مبادرة حقيبة رمضان" word by word.
4. 21.5–27.4s: facts plainly and kindly. Don't oversell the price.
5. 27.4–32s: "زي زمان" with a smile (it answers "ما زي زمان"), then the ask softly. This is the only ask.
6. 36.4–48s: payment lines short and calm, leave room for the cards.
7. 48.5–57s: the proverb is the slowest, most heartfelt read. Pause after "زول واحد", then say the second half as one phrase and land on "والعطاء" as the music resolves.

Pronunciation notes for the less obvious words: عواسة = ʿawāsa, مارق = māreg, الشفع = ash-shuffaʿ, مترجين = mitrajjīn, مرقوا = marrigu (bring out, not "went out"), براكم = barākum, ولا = walla ("or"), برا = barra, صوروا = ṣawwiru, رسلوا = rassilu.

Music: warm oud or nylon guitar with a Sudanese pentatonic feel and soft hand percussion. It thins out at 9.7s, rebuilds from 17s, swells around 48s and resolves on "والعطاء".

Subtitles and on-screen text: write مرّقوا with a shadda and قدُر with a damma so silent viewers read them right. Western digits only, numbers and IBAN as left-to-right runs, never wrap the IBAN.

## Confirm with the organisers before production

1. Bag contents. The VO doesn't name items; the visual shows what the poster shows (grains/legumes, rice, cooking oil, canned goods, dates). Is that representative?
2. Pooling. "لموا حقها مع الأهل والحبان" invites a group to pool 50 dollars for one bag. Is that fine, and do you accept smaller amounts? Fallback: "ورسلوا الفيديو لقروب العيلة".
3. Where bags go. The title card says "لأهلنا في السودان". Are they distributed inside Sudan, or also to displaced families in Egypt?
4. The war line. Fallback with no war mention, same length: "لكن الأيام صعبت، وناس كتار من أهلنا خلوا بيوتهم".
5. First person. "ساهموا معانا" / "رسلوا لينا" speaks from inside the family. If the voice isn't a family member, use "ساهموا بحقيبة" / "ورسلوا الإشعار في الواتساب". Want a small tag under the title like "مبادرة أسرية"?
6. Names on screen. Are وثام، عالية and هبه happy to have their names in a widely forwarded video? Confirm spellings (هبه vs هبة, جبرالله).
7. Western Union. The poster gives no receiver name or country, so the video only says "أو ويسترن يونيون". Send the details if you want it usable.
8. Validity. Are the 50 dollars and all accounts current for Ramadan 1448 (expected to start around 7–8 February 2027)? Want SDG/EGP equivalents on screen?
9. Who records the voice?
